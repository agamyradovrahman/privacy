Privacy Policy

Rug Rally — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.rugrally
Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Last updated: 21 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Rug Rally (the "Game") collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: The Game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. It contains no ads and no in-app purchases, and it does not read your device's advertising identifier. It does collect anonymous device and gameplay information through one analytics service so we can understand how the Game is played and improve its levels.

1. Information collected

We do not operate our own servers and we do not ask you to register. All information is collected automatically by a third-party software development kit (SDK) embedded in the Game:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Anonymous installation identifier A random identifier generated by GameAnalytics on first launch and stored on your device Counting installs and sessions without identifying you. It is not your advertising ID, it is not linked to any account, and it is discarded when you uninstall the Game.
Device information Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, platform, language, game version Diagnosing crashes and performance problems, supporting different devices
Gameplay and app activity Level started, level completed or failed, time spent on a level, number of moves, session length Understanding difficulty balance and improving level design
Crash and error reports Error messages and technical stack traces produced by the Game Finding and fixing bugs
Approximate location Country, derived from the IP address by our provider Aggregated regional statistics only. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Your advertising identifier (GAID) — the Game does not request the AD_ID permission and does not read it
  • Payment or financial information — the Game contains no in-app purchases
  • Social network profile data — the Game contains no social login and no sharing features

3. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, the state of the level you left unfinished, and your sound preference are saved locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services. Information collected by these services is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • GameAnalytics — anonymous gameplay analytics and crash reporting. gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Google Play services — app distribution, updates and integrity checks. policies.google.com/privacy

The Game contains no advertising networks. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with any parties other than the service providers listed above.

5. No advertising identifiers and no tracking

The Game does not display advertisements and does not measure advertising campaigns. It therefore does not read your device's advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other apps or websites.

The only identifier involved is the random installation identifier described in section 1, which exists solely inside this Game. Uninstalling the Game removes it; reinstalling the Game creates a new one, and the two cannot be linked to each other.

6. Children's privacy

Rug Rally is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Analytics data is retained by our service provider in aggregated, anonymous form according to its own retention schedule. Because the Game has no accounts, we hold no personal profile that could identify you individually.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA in California). To exercise these rights, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. Because the data we receive is anonymous, please include your device model and the approximate date you played so that we can locate the relevant records. Uninstalling the Game stops all further data collection.

9. Security

All data transmitted by the Game is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
