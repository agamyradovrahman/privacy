Privacy Policy

Rug Rally — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.rugrally
Effective date: 21 September 2026 · Last updated: 15 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Rug Rally (the "Game") collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: The Game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. It displays no ads and contains no in-app purchases. It collects anonymous device and gameplay information through an analytics service so we can improve its levels, and it uses the Meta (Facebook) SDK, which reads your device's advertising identifier and records app events such as installs and app launches so that we can measure our own advertising campaigns.

1. Information collected

We do not operate our own servers and we do not ask you to register. All information is collected automatically by third-party software development kits (SDKs) embedded in the Game:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Anonymous installation identifier A random identifier generated by GameAnalytics on first launch and stored on your device Counting installs and sessions in our gameplay analytics. It is not linked to any account and is discarded when you uninstall the Game.
Advertising identifier Your device's Google Advertising ID (GAID), read by the Meta SDK Measuring and attributing our advertising campaigns (for example, learning whether an install came from one of our ads)
App events App installs, app launches and sessions, recorded by the Meta SDK Measuring the effectiveness of our advertising campaigns
Device information Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, platform, language, game version Diagnosing crashes and performance problems, supporting different devices, campaign measurement
Gameplay and app activity Level started, level completed or failed, time spent on a level, number of moves, session length Understanding difficulty balance and improving level design
Crash and error reports Error messages and technical stack traces produced by the Game Finding and fixing bugs
Approximate location Country, derived from the IP address by our providers Aggregated regional statistics only. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Payment or financial information — the Game contains no in-app purchases
  • Social network profile data — the Game has no Facebook login and no sharing features

3. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, the state of the level you left unfinished, and your sound preference are saved locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services. Information collected by these services is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • GameAnalytics — anonymous gameplay analytics and crash reporting. gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — measurement and attribution of our advertising campaigns. The Meta SDK collects your device's advertising identifier and app events (such as app installs and app launches) and shares them with Meta Platforms, Inc. facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Google Play services — app distribution, updates and integrity checks. policies.google.com/privacy

The Game does not display advertisements. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with any parties other than the service providers listed above.

5. Advertising identifier and your choices

We advertise the Game on Facebook and Instagram. To learn which of our ads lead to installs, the Meta SDK reads your device's advertising identifier and sends app events to Meta. This information is used only to measure our own campaigns; the Game shows no ads to you.

You can limit this at any time in your device settings: on Android, open Settings > Privacy > Ads (or Settings > Google > Ads) and reset or delete your advertising ID. After you delete it, the Game can no longer read it.

6. Children's privacy

Rug Rally is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Analytics data is retained by GameAnalytics in aggregated, anonymous form, and advertising measurement data is retained by Meta, each according to its own retention schedule. Because the Game has no accounts, we hold no personal profile that could identify you individually.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA in California). To exercise these rights, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. Please include your device model and the approximate date you played so that we can locate the relevant records. You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (see section 5). Uninstalling the Game stops all further data collection.

9. Security

All data transmitted by the Game is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
