Privacy Policy

Cube Filling — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.cubefilling
Effective date: 16 September 2026 · Last updated: 16 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Cube Filling (the "Game") collects, why it is collected, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: The Game has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location. It does collect anonymous device and gameplay information through two analytics services so we can understand how the Game is played and measure our advertising.

1. Information collected

We do not operate our own servers and we do not ask you to register. All information is collected automatically by third-party software development kits (SDKs) embedded in the Game:

CategoryExamplesPurpose
Device and other identifiers Google Advertising ID (GAID), app-set ID, install referrer Measuring advertising campaigns, distinguishing devices in anonymous statistics
Device information Device model, manufacturer, operating system version, screen size, language, app version Diagnosing crashes and performance problems, supporting different devices
Gameplay and app activity Level started, level completed or failed, session length, progression events, error logs Understanding difficulty balance and improving level design
Approximate location Country, derived from the IP address by our providers Aggregated regional statistics only. We do not collect GPS or precise location.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Payment or financial information — the Game contains no in-app purchases
  • Facebook profile data — the Game contains no Facebook Login and no sharing features

3. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, the state of the board you left behind, and your sound and settings preferences are saved locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

The Game uses the following services. Information collected by these services is governed by their own privacy policies:

  • GameAnalytics — anonymous gameplay analytics. gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — app install and app event measurement for our advertising campaigns. The Game uses only app event reporting; it does not display Facebook content, does not offer Facebook Login, and does not read your Facebook profile. facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Google Play services — app distribution, install attribution and integrity checks. policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with any parties other than the service providers listed above.

5. Advertising ID and your choices

The Game reads your device's advertising identifier to measure how effective our advertisements are. You can control this at any time on your device:

  • Reset your advertising ID: Settings → Google → Ads → Reset advertising ID
  • Opt out of ad personalisation: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or Opt out of Ads Personalisation on older Android versions)

After you delete your advertising ID, apps — including this one — can no longer read it.

6. Children's privacy

Cube Filling is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with such information, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Analytics data is retained by our service providers in aggregated, anonymous form according to their own retention schedules. Because the Game has no accounts, we hold no personal profile that could identify you individually.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data, or to object to it (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA in California). To exercise these rights, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days. The fastest way to stop all data collection is to delete your advertising ID as described in section 5 and uninstall the Game.

9. Security

All data transmitted by the Game is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
