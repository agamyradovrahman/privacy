Privacy Policy

Cube Factory — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.cubefactory
Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 29 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Cube Factory (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: The Game has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts or photos. It displays no ads and contains no in-app purchases. To measure how the Game is played and how players find it, the Game uses analytics and attribution services — GameAnalytics, Adjust and the Meta (Facebook) SDK — provided through the TinySauce toolkit of our publishing partner Voodoo SAS. These services receive device identifiers (including the advertising ID), gameplay events, basic device information and approximate location derived from your IP address. Your game progress is saved only on your own device. You can ask us to delete this data at any time — see Data deletion.

1. Information collected

We do not operate servers for the Game and we do not ask you to register. While you play, the services listed in section 4 automatically collect the following data:

DataExamplesPurpose
Device or other identifiers Google Advertising ID, Android app set ID, random installation identifiers Analytics, advertising measurement (attribution of installs to ad campaigns)
App interactions Game sessions and their length, levels started, completed or failed, score Analytics, advertising measurement
Approximate location Country and region derived from your IP address Analytics
Crash logs and diagnostics Error reports, device model, operating system version, app version, language, performance data App functionality, analytics
Install information The store referrer and the ad campaign, if any, that led to the install Advertising measurement

This data is not linked to your name. It is used to understand how the Game is played, to fix problems, and to measure the performance of advertising campaigns for the Game on other apps and websites (for example on Facebook and Instagram). The Game itself does not show advertisements.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Precise (GPS) location
  • Payment or financial information — the Game contains no in-app purchases
  • Social network profile data — the Game has no social login and no sharing features

3. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, the state of the level you left unfinished, and your sound preference are saved locally on your device. This data is never transmitted and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

  • Voodoo SAS — our publishing partner; provides the TinySauce toolkit and has access to the analytics and attribution data described in section 1.
    voodoo.io/privacy
  • GameAnalytics — gameplay analytics (sessions, level progress, device information, errors).
    gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Adjust — install attribution and advertising measurement (advertising ID, install referrer, IP address).
    adjust.com/terms/privacy-policy
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook SDK) — app events and advertising measurement for campaigns on Facebook and Instagram (advertising ID, app events).
    facebook.com/privacy/policy
  • Unity Technologies — the game engine the Game is built with.
    unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play services — app distribution, updates and integrity checks.
    policies.google.com/privacy

We do not sell your personal information. Data is shared only with the partners above, for the purposes described in this policy.

5. Your choices

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings › Google › Ads › Delete advertising ID). After deletion, services can no longer use it to measure advertising.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the Game stops all data collection.
  • Deletion: you can ask us to delete the data collected about you — see section 8.

6. Children's privacy

Cube Factory is not directed at children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child's data has been collected, please contact us and we will delete it.

7. Data retention

Analytics and attribution data is kept by the services in section 4 under their own retention rules (see their privacy policies) and is deleted on request (see section 8). Data saved on your device (see section 3) remains there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.

8. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA in California). To request deletion of the data described in section 1, follow the steps on our Data deletion page. You can remove all data the Game has saved on your device by uninstalling it or by clearing its storage in your device settings. We respond to every request within 30 days.

9. International transfers

The services in section 4 may process data on servers outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States, under the safeguards described in their own privacy policies.

10. Security

All data sent by the Game is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data saved on your device is protected by your device's own security features.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if a future version of the Game adds a new service. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
