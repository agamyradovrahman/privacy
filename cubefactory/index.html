Privacy Policy

Cube Factory — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.cubefactory
Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 28 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Cube Factory (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: The Game has no user accounts and never asks for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. It displays no ads and contains no in-app purchases. The Unity game engine the Game is built with sends a small amount of technical data to Unity Technologies: a random device/installation identifier, basic device information, and crash and performance diagnostics. This data is used only for analytics and to keep the Game working. Your game progress is saved only on your own device. You can ask us to delete this data at any time — see Data deletion.

1. Information collected

We do not operate servers for the Game and we do not ask you to register. The Game itself does not send your data to us. However, the Game is built with the Unity engine, which automatically collects the following technical data and sends it to Unity Technologies:

DataExamplesPurpose
Device or other identifiers A random installation / device identifier generated by Unity Analytics (counting installs and sessions)
Crash logs Error reports when the Game crashes App functionality, analytics
Diagnostics Device model, operating system version, app version, performance data App functionality, analytics

This data is collected automatically while you play and cannot be turned off from inside the Game. It is not linked to your name or any account, and it is not used for advertising.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • Payment or financial information — the Game contains no in-app purchases
  • Social network profile data — the Game has no social login and no sharing features

3. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, the state of the level you left unfinished, and your sound preference are saved locally on your device. This data is never transmitted and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

  • Unity Technologies — game engine; receives the technical data listed in section 1 as our service provider.
    unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Google Play services — app distribution, updates and integrity checks.
    policies.google.com/privacy

The Game does not display advertisements. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone other than the service providers above.

5. Children's privacy

Cube Factory does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child's data has been collected, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention

Technical data collected by Unity is kept by Unity Technologies under its own retention rules (see its privacy policy) and is deleted on request (see section 7). Data saved on your device (see section 3) remains there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.

7. Your rights and data deletion

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA in California). To request deletion of the data described in section 1, follow the steps on our Data deletion page. You can remove all data the Game has saved on your device by uninstalling it or by clearing its storage in your device settings. We respond to every request within 30 days.

8. Security

Technical data sent by the Unity engine is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data saved on your device is protected by your device's own security features.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if a future version of the Game adds a new service. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
