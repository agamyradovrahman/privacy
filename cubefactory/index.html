Privacy Policy

Cube Factory — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.cubefactory
Effective date: 24 October 2026 · Last updated: 24 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the mobile game Cube Factory (the "Game") collects, why, and the choices you have. By installing or using the Game, you agree to the practices described here.

In short: The Game does not collect, store on our servers, or share any personal information. It has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or location. It displays no ads, contains no in-app purchases, and includes no analytics, advertising, or attribution SDKs. Your progress is saved only on your own device.

1. Information collected

We do not collect any information from you. We do not operate servers for the Game, we do not ask you to register, and the Game does not send your data to us or to any third party. The Game does not read your device's advertising identifier.

2. Information we do not collect

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or postal address
  • Contacts, calendar, photos, camera, microphone, or files on your device
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • Gameplay analytics, usage statistics, or crash reports
  • Payment or financial information — the Game contains no in-app purchases
  • Social network profile data — the Game has no social login and no sharing features

3. Data stored only on your device

Your level progress, the state of the level you left unfinished, and your sound preference are saved locally on your device. This data is never transmitted to us and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

4. Third-party services

The Game does not include any third-party analytics, advertising, or attribution services. It is distributed through Google Play, which may collect information as described in its own privacy policy:

  • Google Play services — app distribution, updates and integrity checks.
    policies.google.com/privacy

The Game does not display advertisements. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone.

5. Children's privacy

Cube Factory does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

6. Data retention

Because the Game collects no personal information and has no accounts, we hold no data about you. Data saved on your device (see section 3) remains there until you uninstall the Game or clear its data in your device settings.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data (for example under the GDPR in the European Economic Area, or the CCPA in California). Since we do not collect personal data, there is no data for us to provide or delete. You can remove all data the Game has saved on your device by uninstalling it or by clearing its storage in your device settings. If you have any questions, email us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Security

The Game does not transmit your personal data. Data saved on your device is protected by your device's own security features.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time — for example, if a future version of the Game adds a new service. The revised version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Significant changes will also be noted in the Game's store listing.
