Data Deletion Request

Cube Factory — published by AGPARR GAMES
Package name: com.agparrgames.cubefactory
Last updated: 29 October 2026

Cube Factory has no user accounts. The data related to you that leaves your device is the analytics and attribution data collected by GameAnalytics, Adjust and the Meta (Facebook) SDK through our publishing partner Voodoo SAS (device identifiers including the advertising ID, gameplay events, approximate location, crash logs and diagnostics — see our Privacy Policy). You can ask us to delete it at any time.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to rahmanagamyradov1111@gmail.com with the subject "Cube Factory – Data deletion request".
  2. In the email, include your device model (for example "Samsung Galaxy A54"), the approximate date you installed the Game and, if you can, your advertising ID (Android: Settings › Google › Ads). Because the data is not linked to your name, this helps us find it.
  3. We will forward your request to our partners (Voodoo, GameAnalytics, Adjust and Meta), have the data deleted, and confirm by email within 30 days.

Delete data saved on your device

Your level progress and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them, uninstall the Game, or open Settings › Apps › Cube Factory › Storage › Clear data on your device. You can also delete your advertising ID in Settings › Google › Ads.
