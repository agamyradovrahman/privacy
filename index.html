Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how AGPARR Games ("we", "us") handles information when you play our mobile game Fill Balance (the "Game").

Information We Collect

We do not ask for or collect any personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number. The Game uses third-party analytics services that automatically collect limited, non-personal information, including:

  • Device information (device model, operating system version, language, screen size)
  • Gameplay data (levels started and completed, session length, in-game progress)
  • Approximate region (derived from IP address; the IP address itself is not stored by us)
  • Advertising identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID), used in aggregated form for analytics and measuring marketing campaigns

Third-Party Services

The Game uses the following third-party services, which have their own privacy policies:

  • GameAnalytics — gameplay analytics. https://gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Meta (Facebook) SDK — measurement of marketing campaigns. https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy

How We Use Information

The collected information is used only to understand how the Game is played, fix bugs, improve the Game, and measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The Game does not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us and we will delete it.

Data Retention and Your Rights

Analytics data is retained by our third-party providers according to their own policies. Depending on your region (e.g., EU/GDPR, California/CCPA), you may have rights to access or request deletion of your data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below. You can also reset or delete your device advertising identifier in your device settings.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
